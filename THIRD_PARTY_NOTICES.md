# Subscription service icons

Netflix, Spotify, YouTube, iCloud and Dropbox SVG paths are from
[Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons).
The OpenAI path is from [Simple Icons 11.0.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.0.0/icons/openai.svg).
Simple Icons is distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners and identify subscription services; no endorsement is implied.

The icons are bundled locally so the picker and saved subscriptions work offline.
