## 繁體中文

**裝置內資料。** 毋須註冊或登入。App 將你輸入嘅記帳資料（包括收支、分類、預算、信用卡帳期、訂閱、備註及選擇附上嘅收據圖片）、設定同 App PIN 驗證資料儲存在裝置嘅 App 儲存空間。App 不會將這些資料上傳至營運者、連接你的銀行帳戶，或自動同步到 iCloud。App PIN 用於鎖定介面，並非帳簿加密功能。

**連線及外部服務。** 信用卡目錄會向 Google 公開試算表取得產品資料（HTTPS），不會把你的帳簿、信用卡輸入或裝置記錄放入請求。服務供應商可能收到 IP 位址、連線及標準請求資訊，並按其[私隱政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)處理。如果你開啟自訂的訂閱管理連結，該網站可按其自身政策處理資料。App 沒有廣告追蹤或內置分析 SDK。

**裝置權限。** 如你選擇拍攝或加入收據圖片，系統可能要求相機或相片存取權限。通知權限用於排程裝置本機提醒，不需推播帳戶或伺服器；可在 App 或系統設定關閉。

**備份及刪除。** 你可選擇經 iOS 分享選單或「檔案」匯出備份或報告。由你選取嘅目的地（例如 iCloud Drive）同作業系統備份各自按其設定運作；App 不會主動上傳。你可透過 App 資料設定刪除帳簿或刪除 App；已匯出檔案、分享副本及系統備份，須在相應位置另行管理。

如有疑問，請聯絡 `<PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL>`。更新後的政策會在此頁公佈。

## English

**Data on your device.** No account or sign-in is required. The app stores entries, categories, budgets, card schedules, subscriptions, notes, optional receipt images, settings, and the app PIN verifier in the app's local storage. It does not upload those records to the operator, connect to a bank account, or automatically sync to iCloud. The PIN locks the app interface; it does not encrypt the ledger.

**Network requests and third parties.** The card catalog makes an HTTPS request to a public Google spreadsheet for product data. The request does not include your ledger, entered card details, or device records. The provider may receive an IP address, connection information, and standard request metadata, subject to its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you follow a subscription management link you supplied, the destination applies its own privacy policy. The app contains no advertising tracking or analytics SDK.

**Device permissions.** The operating system may request camera or photo access if you choose to attach a receipt image. Notification permission is used for on-device reminders, not server push. You can turn reminders off in the app or in system settings.

**Backups and deletion.** You decide where to save or share exported backups or reports using the iOS share sheet or Files. A destination you choose (such as iCloud Drive) and operating-system backups follow their own settings; the app does not proactively upload those files. You can delete ledger data in the app's data settings or remove the app. Manage exported files, copies you shared, and system backups separately.
