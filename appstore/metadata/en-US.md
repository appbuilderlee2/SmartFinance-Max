# App Store Connect: English (U.S.)

**App name**: SmartFinance Max

**Subtitle**: Budget, Expenses & Bills

**Promotional text**: Track expenses, budgets, card due dates, and renewals in one place. No account is required, and you can export your own backup.

**Description**

SmartFinance Max helps you keep track of everyday spending and recurring bills. Record a quick lunch expense, see where your money went this month, or check when a subscription renews.

- **Record income and expenses.** Organize entries with categories, tags, notes, and optional receipt images.
- **Review your month.** Browse a calendar and transaction history, compare spending with category budgets, and export CSV reports.
- **Manage cards and subscriptions.** Track statement dates, payment due dates, and renewals. There is no bank login or bank-account connection.
- **Set local reminders.** Configure bookkeeping, card, subscription, and budget reminders, and view upcoming notifications in Settings. Notifications require system permission.
- **Keep control of your records.** Core bookkeeping works offline. You can use an optional app PIN and privacy masks, and export or restore a backup yourself. The app does not automatically sync to iCloud.
